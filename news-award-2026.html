favehotels wins best budget hotel brand at the Indonesia travel awards 2026

date16 nov 2026
locationJakarta, Indonesia
issued byfavehotels group

favehotels has been named best budget hotel brand at the Indonesia travel awards 2026, recognised for consistent value, bright rooms and a friendly guest experience across the country.

the award celebrates brands that deliver a dependable, good-value stay at scale. judges pointed to favehotels' steady rooms, quick direct booking and warm service as reasons for the win, chosen from more than 40 shortlisted hotel brands.

"this one belongs to every fave team member who makes a bright, easy stay happen, every day," said a spokesperson for favehotels group.

favehotels will carry the momentum into 2026 with its refreshed brand look, new family rooms in 18 hotels, and more openings planned across Indonesia and Malaysia.

about favehotels

favehotels is the smart, affordable brand of archipelago, offering bright and easy stays across Indonesia and Malaysia.
