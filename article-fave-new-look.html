favehotels is getting a glow-up. this june we rolled out a brighter new brand look — bold colours, hand-drawn patterns and a warmer welcome — across favehotels in Indonesia and Malaysia.

why a new look

a lot has changed since fave first opened its doors. our guests travel differently, book differently and expect more from a smart, affordable stay. the refresh keeps everything people already love about fave — easy, friendly, good value — and makes it feel brighter and more unmistakably ours.

the goal was simple: a look that feels like a holiday the moment you walk in, whether that's a quick city stopover or a long weekend by the pool.

colours & patterns

at the heart of the new look is a confident violet paired with a fresh lime green, grounded by soft neutrals. the two work together across everything from room keys to the booking screen you're reading this on.

the hand-drawn circular patterns are inspired by batik and the textiles of the regions we call home. you'll spot them framing our pages, tucked into corners, and printed across signage and welcome cards.

what changes for guests

mostly, it's the little things that add up: clearer signage, brighter rooms, friendlier wording, and a booking flow that's quicker to get through. rates, rooms and the warm fave welcome stay exactly as they are — just easier and nicer to use.

the rollout

the new look is live across our websites now, and arrives in hotels through the rest of the year — lobbies and signage first, then rooms and printed welcome kits. thanks for coming along as fave gets a little brighter.
